FAQ

Questions

What exactly do I get?

A full access Microsoft account that owns Minecraft Java and Bedrock. You get the email and password, so you can change both and take over the account completely.

What is the difference between names and accounts?

On the Names page you buy a specific rare username. On the Accounts page you buy fresh accounts (with a free name change), accounts with a cape, or MedalMC bundles.

How fast is delivery?

Instantly. As soon as your payment clears, the login details appear on your order page and in your email.

Can I move a name to my own account?

Minecraft names are tied to an account, so you get the account with the name. Change the email and password and use it as your main account.

What if I lose access?

Every account has a 14-day warranty. If you lose access through no fault of your own, we replace the account or refund you.

Which payment methods do you accept?

iDEAL, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Visa and Mastercard, via Mollie and Stripe.
